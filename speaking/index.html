Bio

Please feel free to copy and paste this for your event listing. You can also download a high-res image for your event materials.

Dave is a director and developer at Lamp Bristol, a software consultancy. He's been writing software commercially for over two decades in many languages including C, Python, Java and PHP. Dave specialises in static analysis, automated testing and code review — helping teams keep large, long-lived codebases safe to change.

Dave is keen to pass on his knowledge. He speaks at conferences and user groups and also is the organiser of PHP-SW.

He is also the author of a couple of libraries:

  • SARB (static analysis results baseliner)
  • PHP Language Extensions Library

When not busy coding Dave enjoys scuba diving and running.
