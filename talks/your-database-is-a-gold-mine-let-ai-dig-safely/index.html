Your Database Is a Gold Mine. Let AI Dig Safely.

ai conference talk

Abstract

Imagine anyone in the business asking questions of the database like they're talking to a person. No SQL. No ticket. No waiting for a developer.

We built it: an MCP with read only DB access that hid PII. Every client who has tried it is hooked.

But AI also finds fool's gold. I'll show how to build one, how to stop confident wrong answers (well, most of them), and how to turn the best questions into trusted reports.
