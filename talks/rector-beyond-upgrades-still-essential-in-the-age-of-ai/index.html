Rector Beyond Upgrades: Still Essential in the Age of AI

rector ai conference talk

Abstract

Rector is best known for upgrades, but it goes much further. And no, AI coding agents haven't made it redundant.

Ask an agent to change 500 files and it might miss a few. Ask it to write a Rector rule and the change is made the same way everywhere, every time.

We'll look at automating repetitive tasks, enforcing standards and creating custom rules, by hand or with AI. You get the flexibility of AI with results you can trust.
